# Preview Of The End Result

Humans are visual creatures. When people can see the result while they make it, they find mistakes before the result leaves the screen.

## When To Show A Preview

Show the end result when the work makes a tangible thing that people see or hold outside the app.

- Examples are a printed check-in tag, a shipping label, an invoice, a rich text document, and an outgoing email or message.
- Put the preview next to the fields.

## When Not To Show A Preview

- Do not preview a record that lives only in the app. When people only update the information of a contact, a preview of a contact card adds nothing that the fields do not show.
- When the same contact data goes on a printed business card, preview the printed card.
- Do not add a preview only to fill space. A preview that does not show a real thing takes attention from the fields. Put computed facts, such as a count or an estimate, in the form footer. [[form-layout#footer]] gives the rule.

## Make It Look Like The Thing

- Give the preview the shape, the proportions, the paper or screen surface, the type hierarchy, and the position of each value of the real thing.
- An invoice editor shows the invoice page. A rich text editor shows the formatted text. A check-in tag form shows the tag.
- The preview is a good place for a signature element. [[signature-elements]] gives the rules.

## Keep It Live

- Update the preview while people type.
- Show a quiet placeholder for each empty answer, so that the layout of the thing stays visible.
