# Images And Finishing Details

Finishing details come after the structure works. Each detail must support the task or the voice of the page.

## Images

- Use images at their intended size.
- Do not enlarge small assets until they become soft.
- Do not reduce detailed screenshots until their text becomes unreadable. When the target area is smaller, use a narrower capture, a partial capture, or a simplified illustration.
- Make sure that text over an image has sufficient contrast. Use an overlay, an image treatment, or a controlled text surface.
- Constrain user-uploaded images in fixed containers. Account for transparent backgrounds and backgrounds that match the page.

## Empty States

- Design an empty state as a first-use experience that teaches the interface, not only a message that says "nothing here". [[ux-writing#empty-states]] gives the copy.
- Remove filters, tabs, or actions that have no purpose before content exists.

## Decoration

- Add decorative accents only after the hierarchy works.
- A border, a quote mark, an illustration, or a background detail must support the voice of the page.
- A signature element from the domain is not decoration. [[signature-elements]] gives the rules.
